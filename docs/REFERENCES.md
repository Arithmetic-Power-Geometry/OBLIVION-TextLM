# Primary theoretical references

## OBLIVION

Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). **OBLIVION: Query-Relative Neural Computation by Verified Semantic
Obligation Retirement** (Version V1). Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22172608

This is the primary architectural reference for Semantic Obligation State, obligation birth,
Verified Net Discharge, counterfactual discharge, adaptive obligation budgets, and the SHC-aware
net-gain rule implemented by OBLIVION TextLM.

## Task-Semantic Images (TSI)

Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). **Beyond Complete Compilation: Task-Semantic Images and the
Complexity of Computing Only What a Query Can See** (Version V1). Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22160359

TSI supplies the task-visible semantic perspective and the warning that small semantic images do
not by themselves guarantee cheap realizability or composition.

## Semantic Lifetime Width (SLW)

Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). **When Can a Computation Forget? Semantic Lifetime Width and
Query-Relative State Retirement** (Version V1). Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22162568

SLW supplies the temporal future-equivalence and certificate-based retirement perspective that
motivates conservative KEEP behavior when safe retirement is not established.

## Semantic Hardness Conservation (SHC)

Akhtar, M. A. K. (2026). **Semantic Hardness Conservation: An Operational Normal Form for
Exact Reasoning After Compression** (Version V1). Zenodo.
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22164419

SHC supplies the operational audit separating encoding, realizability, separation/certification,
and transformation costs and motivates explicit accounting rather than equating compression
with computational progress.
